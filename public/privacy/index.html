Privacy policy: Nestor review

Last updated November 20, 2026

Nestor review is a personal tool used only by its author, Bill Huang. It is not offered to anyone else.

With the author's permission it reads his own Gmail and Google Calendar, read-only, to write him a weekly summary. It cannot send, change or delete mail or events.

It runs on the author's own computer. To sort the week's mail, message subjects, senders and short previews are sent to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude). The finished summary is kept on that computer and sent to the author over Telegram. Nothing is sold, shared for any other purpose or used for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's "Third-party apps & services" page.
